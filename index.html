Our product

Roundtrip — Australia's best digital learner logbook app.

We built and operate Roundtrip, the highest-rated learner driver logbook app in Australia, used in NSW and Tasmania. It replaces paper and legacy digital logbooks with a modern, research-backed platform focused on road safety outcomes. not just tracking hours.

Our product

Virtuous Cycle — make regular bike riding fun and easy.

Virtuous Cycle encourages regular bike riding through route search, ride metrics, awards, and a learning centre. It offers up to three route variations per destination to cater for riders of varying ability levels. A winner of the Transport for NSW Active Transport Bike Riding Innovation Challenge.

About us

Research-led. Brisbane-based. Since 2015.

Eat More Pixels is a Brisbane app company specialising in mobile app development. We combine technical expertise with research to build great apps that deliver real-world impact.

Roundtrip started as a PhD research project on safer driving in young drivers. That curiosity-first mindset still shapes everything we build.
