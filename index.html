Our product

Roundtrip — Australia's best digital learner logbook app.

We own and operate Roundtrip, the highest-rated learner driver logbook app in Australia, used officially in NSW and Tasmania. It replaces paper and legacy digital logbooks with a modern, research-backed platform focused on road safety outcomes. Available for rollout to other jurisdictions.

Visit roundtripapp.com
Our product

Plates Plus — Tasmania's official learner logbook app.

Plates Plus is a white-labelled version of Roundtrip, built for the Tasmanian Government. It lets Tasmanian learner drivers digitally record and track their supervised driving hours, replacing paper logbooks with a modern app tailored to Tasmania's GDL requirements.

Official TAS Logbook White-label iOS & Android
Visit platesplus.tas.gov.au

Roundtrip is available for rollout to other states, territories and countries.

Our product

Virtuous Cycle — make regular bike riding fun and easy.

Virtuous Cycle encourages regular bike riding through route search, ride metrics, awards, and a learning centre. It offers up to three route variations per destination to cater for riders of varying ability levels. A winner of the NSW Government Active Transport Bike Riding Innovation Challenge.

iOS App Built for NSW cyclists
Visit virtuouscycle.app
